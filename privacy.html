Privacy Policy

FoldFail · Last updated: November 7, 2026
In short: FoldFail does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. Everything happens on your device.

Data we collect

None. FoldFail has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not connect to the internet.

Photos access

If you allow it, FoldFail reads your photo library only on your device to find the newest screenshot of your home screen, which is then shown under the fake crack. The selected image is saved only inside the app on your device and is deleted when you remove it in the app or delete the app. No photos are uploaded anywhere. You can change or revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Settings

Your prank settings (for example sound and trigger options) are stored only on your device.

Children

FoldFail does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes
